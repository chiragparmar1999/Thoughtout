# ThoughtOut Instagram Voice Profile

Brand: ThoughtOut
Primary goal: promote open mics, performer showcases, creator events and audience ticket sales.

Voice:
- Energetic, human, local and creator-first.
- Hindi/Hinglish is preferred for Instagram; use English for key event terms and CTAs.
- Keep copy punchy and easy to say aloud.
- Avoid corporate/AI-sounding language, generic motivational filler and fake hype.
- Never invent dates, times, venues, prices, discounts, prizes, scarcity, performance claims or metrics.
- Use only confirmed event/offer information supplied by ThoughtOut.
- Preserve performer names and supplied handles exactly.
- CTA should be specific: book tickets, register as performer, DM a keyword, or attend the event, only when that action is actually available.
- For performer promotions, make benefits clear and prominent.
- For reels, prioritize a strong first 3 seconds, concrete details, emotional payoff and a clean CTA.
- For hashtags, follow current Instagram limits and use only relevant tags.

Safety/accuracy:
- Do not claim an event is sold out, limited, confirmed, sponsored, or available unless the source information says so.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, audience numbers or performance results.
